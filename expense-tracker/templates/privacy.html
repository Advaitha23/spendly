Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. When you use the Service, we collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic usage information, such as when you log in, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide to operate the Service, including:

  • Displaying your expenses, categories, and spending summaries
  • Authenticating your account and keeping it secure
  • Improving the features and reliability of Spendly
  • Communicating important updates about your account or the Service

We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers, such as hosting and database services, to operate the Service. These providers process data only as necessary to deliver their service to us and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
